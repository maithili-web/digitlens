DigitLens

Draw a digit from 0 to 9. A convolutional neural network reads it as you draw.

Draw large and centered for the best result.
–
Waiting for a drawing

    The network never sees your raw drawing. Your strokes are cropped, scaled to fit a 20×20 box and centered in a 28×28 image, the same format as the MNIST training set.

    Model: 3-layer CNN with batch norm, trained on MNIST with PyTorch. Served with Flask.
